# MOBA: claim audit

One row per claim a paper could make, with its evidence class and the artefact it
rests on. Classes are defined in [../PROVENANCE.md](../PROVENANCE.md):
- **M** measured, with a recoverable artefact;
- **Q** qualitative;
- **I** inferred;
- **A** author-confirmed;
- **U** unverified.

**Nothing below M goes into a paper as a result. Nothing at U goes in until it is closed.**
Fill a row when the measurement happens, not when the paper is written.

**Source aliases:**
- **Runs** = `results/moba/<run_id>/` (W&B exports; cite the directory, never a link);
- **Tab** = `results/moba/tables/*.md` (generated by `results/moba/make_figures.py`);
- **Dream** = `results/moba/dream/`;
- **Man** = `artifacts/moba/MANIFEST.json`;
- **Prov** = `../PROVENANCE.md`;
- **Arch** = `../WM_ARCHITECTURE.md`;
- **CF** = `../COUNTERFACTUALS.md`.

**Standing caveat on every WM row:** part of each dreamed state is filled in by
hand-coded rules R1–R10 (spawns, creep stats, level and level-derived stats,
counters, targets, clamps), not by the learned model (Arch § Rules vs learned).
Any claim about "the model predicts X" is about the learned part only.

## Method claims that need a control before use

| Claim | Missing control |
|---|---|
| "E6b is better/worse than E5" | ≥2 seeds per arm; a matched wall-clock comparison with seeds |
| "Effect explained %" as action understanding | A held-out counterfactual evaluation (forks from games 900–999), and the untrained-model and E5 baselines on the same forks |
| "Dream reward slope" as policy quality | Real-game evaluation of every sweep config, not only the best (R2 shows dream reward is not a proxy) |
| Jitter/circle rubric metrics as "realism" | The author's own pairwise judgments (`../WM_FEEDBACK_RL.md`) to calibrate them |
